How record label advances work: recoupment explained for independent artists.

1. An advance is paid back out of your royalties.
2. Example: a $50K advance at a 20% royalty needs $250K in revenue to recoup.
3. Cross-collateral lets one release pay for another.
4. A presale funds a release with no recoupment.

The fans who pay up front are the investors with no strings.
