Artist merch rights explained: what a label means when it offers to handle your merch.

1. In a 360 or merch deal, the label can sell your designs and pay you a share.
2. In your own store, the margin is yours.
3. Bundle merch with the music for fans already buying.
4. Every order is a buyer you can email next release.

Own the store and you own the list that comes with it.
