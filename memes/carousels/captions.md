## 1 fan beats 1,000 streams (one-fan-beats-1000-streams-decoded)

How independent artists get paid before streaming: why one presale order beats a year of plays.

1. Spotify pays nothing on a track until it hits 1,000 streams in 12 months.
2. A presale pays the moment the fan checks out.
3. Price it as a ladder so every fan has a way in.
4. Send it to your email list 48 hours before anyone else.

Streams are discovery. The money comes from fans you can reach directly.

## Your money on a leash (your-money-on-a-leash-decoded)

How record label advances work: recoupment explained for independent artists.

1. An advance is paid back out of your royalties.
2. Example: a $50K advance at a 20% royalty needs $250K in revenue to recoup.
3. Cross-collateral lets one release pay for another.
4. A presale funds a release with no recoupment.

The fans who pay up front are the investors with no strings.

## The masters line, decoded (dont-worry-about-the-masters-decoded)

What owning your masters means for independent artists, and how to keep them.

1. The master is the recording itself, and its owner controls it.
2. A license with an end date beats signing it over.
3. The master owner approves syncs and collects that side of the fee.
4. Whoever pays for the recording usually owns it.

Fund the record with your fans and the masters stay yours.
